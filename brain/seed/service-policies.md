## Fixed Deposit Premature Withdrawal

A fixed deposit can be closed before maturity in the AU 0101 app or at any branch.
Premature withdrawal penalty: 1% is deducted from the interest rate applicable for the period the deposit actually ran.
Interest is paid at the rate applicable for the period the deposit was held, less the 1% penalty.
Deposits that have run for less than 7 days earn no interest.
Tax-saver fixed deposits cannot be closed before the 5-year lock-in ends.

## Fixed Deposit Auto-Renewal

If auto-renewal is chosen, the deposit renews on maturity for the same tenure at the rate applicable on the renewal date.
Auto-renewal can be switched on or off any time before maturity in the AU 0101 app or at a branch.

## Loan Foreclosure and Part-Payment

Floating-rate loans to individuals for non-business purposes: no foreclosure or part-payment charges (RBI).
For other loans, the foreclosure charge is set out in that loan's product terms.
The exact foreclosure amount is given by the loan team as a foreclosure letter valid for the stated date.

## EMI Bounce and Late Payment Charges

EMI bounce (auto-debit returned): Rs 500 plus GST per instance.
Late payment charge: 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount.
Keep enough balance in the repayment account one day before the EMI date, because the auto-debit is presented early in the morning.

## Failed Transaction Turnaround Times (RBI)

ATM cash not dispensed but account debited: automatic reversal within 5 working days of the transaction. If late, the bank pays Rs 100 per day of delay to the customer.
Card payment at a shop (POS) debited but not completed: automatic reversal within 5 working days. Compensation Rs 100 per day after that.
Online card payment debited but not completed: automatic reversal within 5 working days. Compensation Rs 100 per day after that.
UPI or IMPS transfer debited but not credited to the beneficiary: automatic reversal by the next working day. Compensation Rs 100 per day after that.
These timelines follow RBI's harmonisation of turnaround time for failed transactions (September 2019).

## Unauthorised Transactions and Fraud

Block the card immediately through the AU 0101 app, net banking, phone banking or this assistant, and report the transaction.
Customer liability is zero if the unauthorised electronic transaction is reported within 3 working days of receiving the bank's alert (RBI).
After a report, the bank credits the disputed amount to the account as a shadow reversal within 10 working days, while the case is investigated (RBI).
Never share an OTP, PIN, CVV or password with anyone, including people claiming to be from the bank.
Cyber fraud can also be reported to the national cyber crime helpline 1930 or at cybercrime.gov.in.

## Card Controls

Debit and credit cards can be switched on or off separately for ATM withdrawals, shop payments, online payments, tap-to-pay (contactless) and international use, and limits can be set for each (RBI direction on enhancing the security of card transactions, 2020).
Controls can be changed in the AU 0101 app, net banking, phone banking or with this assistant.
International use is off by default on new cards and must be switched on before travelling abroad.
A temporarily blocked card can be unblocked any time. A permanently blocked card cannot be reactivated; a replacement card is issued.

## Credit Card Payments and Minimum Due

Minimum amount due: 5% of the total amount due, or Rs 200, whichever is higher, plus any EMIs and overdue amounts.
Payments made by UPI, net banking or NEFT usually reflect on the card within 1 to 2 working days.
Paying only the minimum due avoids a late fee, but interest is charged on the unpaid balance.

## Complaints and Escalation

Complaints can be raised by phone, email, the AU 0101 app, or at any branch, and get a reference number.
Most complaints are resolved within 7 working days; card disputes and chargebacks can take up to 45 days.
If the customer is not satisfied, the complaint can be escalated to the Principal Nodal Officer of AU Small Finance Bank.
If there is no reply within 30 days, or the customer is not satisfied with the reply, a complaint can be filed with the RBI Ombudsman at cms.rbi.org.in (RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme 2021).

## Personal Loan Documents

Salaried applicants: PAN card, Aadhaar or another address proof, salary slips for the last 3 months, and bank statements for the last 6 months.
Self-employed applicants: PAN card, Aadhaar or another address proof, income tax returns for the last 2 years, and bank statements for the last 6 months.
The final interest rate depends on the applicant's credit profile, income and loan amount.

## Salary Credits

Salary can be credited to any AU savings account. Employers can also open salary accounts for their staff through AU's corporate salary programme.
